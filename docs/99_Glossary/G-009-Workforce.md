# G-009: Workforce

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Project:** JKR Site Diary Platform

## Definition

Workforce represents all labour resources assigned to perform Activities on site.

## Characteristics

- Linked to Activities.
- Recorded daily.
- Used for reporting.
