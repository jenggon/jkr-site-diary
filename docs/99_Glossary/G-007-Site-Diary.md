# G-007: Site Diary

## Definition

The Site Diary is the official daily operational record of project execution.

It captures site activities, workforce, weather, progress and operational events.

---

## Characteristics

- Daily record.
- Historical document.
- Immutable after approval.
- Audit evidence.

---

## Ownership

Owned by Zon Operasi.
