# G-006: Open Activity

## Definition

An Open Activity is an Activity that has been started but not yet completed.

Open Activities continue across Site Diary entries until officially closed.

---

## Characteristics

- Active.
- Linked to one Task.
- Appears in daily Site Diary.
- May span multiple days.

---

## Ownership

Owned by Zon Operasi.
