# G-005: Activity

## Definition

An Activity is the smallest executable work performed on site under a Task.

Activities represent actual site execution and are recorded in the Site Diary.

---

## Characteristics

- Belongs to one Task.
- Executed by Workforce.
- Records actual work.
- Produces Progress.

---

## Ownership

Owned by Zon Operasi.
