# G-008: Progress

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Project:** JKR Site Diary Platform

## Definition

Progress represents the measurable completion status of project work performed through Activities.

Progress is accumulated from operational execution rather than planning data.

---

## Characteristics

- Derived from Activities.
- Linked to Tasks.
- Historical.
- Traceable.
