# G-004: Task

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Project:** JKR Site Diary Platform

## Definition

A Task is the smallest schedulable work package imported from the Approved Program Kerja.

Tasks are the primary operational units used to organise activities, workforce allocation and progress recording.

---

## Characteristics

- Has a unique identifier (UID).
- Belongs to one Program Kerja.
- May contain multiple Activities.
- May be planned or completed.

---

## Ownership

Originates from Zon Penjadualan.

Consumed by Zon Operasi.
