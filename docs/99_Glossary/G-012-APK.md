# G-012: APK

## Definition

APK (Arahan Perubahan Kerja), also known as Variation Order (VO), is an approved instruction that modifies the original scope of work.

Approved APKs may introduce new operational planning that is incorporated through a new Programme Revision.

---

## Characteristics

- Controlled change.
- Traceable.
- Versioned.
- Historically preserved.

---

## Ownership

Owned by Zon Penjadualan.

Consumed by Zon Operasi after approval.
