# G-010: AHI

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Project:** JKR Site Diary Platform

## Definition

AHI (Activity Header Information) is the primary operational registration record created before work execution.

It defines the operational identity of an Activity.

---

## Characteristics

- Created before execution.
- Unique identifier.
- Parent of operational records.

---

## Ownership

Owned by Zon Operasi.

---

## Notes

Detailed behaviour is defined in the Business Rules.
