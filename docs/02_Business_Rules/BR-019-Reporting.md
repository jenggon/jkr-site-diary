# BR-019: Reporting

**Project:** JKR Site Diary Platform
**Version:** 1.0.0

Status: Locked

Priority: Medium

## Purpose

Generate operational reports.

## Rule Statement

Reports shall only use approved operational data.

Historical reports shall reproduce the original operational state.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Report generated.
- Historical report reproduced.
