# BR-008: Progress

**Project:** JKR Site Diary Platform
**Version:** 1.0.0

Status: Locked

Priority: High

## Purpose

Define Progress recording.

## Rule Statement

Progress shall be derived from completed Activities.

Progress cannot exceed planned work.

Historical Progress records shall remain immutable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Progress recorded.
- Progress accumulated.
- Progress reported.
