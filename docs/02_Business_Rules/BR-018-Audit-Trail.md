# BR-018: Audit Trail

**Project:** JKR Site Diary Platform
**Version:** 1.0.0

Status: Locked

Priority: Critical

## Purpose

Maintain complete audit history.

## Rule Statement

Every business transaction shall generate an Audit Trail.

Audit records shall be immutable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Audit record generated.
- Historical record preserved.
