# BR-009: Workforce

Status: Active

Priority: High

## Purpose

Define Workforce recording.

## Rule Statement

Every Workforce record shall be associated with at least one Activity.

Daily Workforce information shall be stored within the corresponding Site Diary.

Historical Workforce records shall remain immutable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Workforce recorded.
- Workforce linked to Activity.
- Workforce reported.
