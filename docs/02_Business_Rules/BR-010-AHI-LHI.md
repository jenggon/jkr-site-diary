# BR-010: AHI / LHI

Status: Active

Priority: High

## Purpose

Define operational registration.

## Rule Statement

Every operational Activity shall be registered through an AHI.

An LHI shall reference one AHI and record the physical execution location.

AHI identifiers shall remain immutable.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AHI created.
- LHI linked.
- Operational registration completed.
