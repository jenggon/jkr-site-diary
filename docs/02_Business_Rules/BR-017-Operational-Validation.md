# BR-017: Operational Validation

**Project:** JKR Site Diary Platform
**Version:** 1.0.0

Status: Locked

Priority: High

## Purpose

Validate operational input.

## Rule Statement

Mandatory operational information shall be validated before submission.

Invalid records shall not be committed.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Validation passed.
- Validation rejected.
