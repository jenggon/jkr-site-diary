# BR-016: APK / Variation Order

Status: Active

Priority: High

## Purpose

Manage approved scope changes.

## Rule Statement

Approved APK shall initiate a new Programme Revision.

Existing historical operational records shall remain unchanged.

## Acceptance Criteria

- APK approved.
- New revision created.
- Historical records preserved.
