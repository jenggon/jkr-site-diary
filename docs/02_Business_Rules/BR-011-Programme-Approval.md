# BR-011: Programme Approval

Status: Active

Priority: Critical

## Purpose

Define Programme approval behaviour.

## Rule Statement

Only authorised users may approve a Programme Revision.

Approval immediately establishes the revision as the official operational baseline.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Authorised approval.
- Revision status updated.
- Program Kerja generated.
