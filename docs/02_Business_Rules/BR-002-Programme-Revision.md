# BR-002: Programme Revision

**Project:** JKR Site Diary Platform
**Version:** 1.0.0

Status: Locked

Priority: Critical

## Purpose

Define Programme Revision behaviour.

## Rule Statement

Every Programme Revision belongs to exactly one Programme.

Only one revision may hold Approved status.

Approval archives the previous revision automatically.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Draft revision created.
- Revision approved.
- Previous revision archived.
