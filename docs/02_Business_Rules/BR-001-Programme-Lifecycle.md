# BR-001: Programme Lifecycle

Status: Active

Priority: Critical

## Purpose

Define the lifecycle of a Programme.

## Rule Statement

Every Programme shall exist throughout the project lifecycle and may contain multiple Programme Revisions.

A Programme cannot exist without an approved owner.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Programme created.
- Programme updated.
- Programme archived.
