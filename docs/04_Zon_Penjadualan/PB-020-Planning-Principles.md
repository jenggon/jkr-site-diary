# PB-020 — Planning Principles

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Project:** JKR Site Diary Platform

## Status

Locked

---

# Principles

1. Planning precedes execution.

2. Planning is immutable after publication.

3. Operational execution shall never modify planning data.

4. Every Task originates from an approved Programme Revision.

5. Program Kerja is the operational contract between planning and execution.

6. UID is the official integration key.

7. Historical Revisions remain available for audit.
