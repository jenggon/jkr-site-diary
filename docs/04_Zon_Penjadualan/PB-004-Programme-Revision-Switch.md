# PB-004 — Programme Revision Switch

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Project:** JKR Site Diary Platform

## Status

Locked

---

# Purpose

Activate a newly approved Programme Revision.

---

# Rules

- Only one Revision may be Active.
- Previous Revision becomes Historical.
- Historical Revisions remain immutable.
- Existing operational records are preserved.

---

# Output

New operational baseline.
