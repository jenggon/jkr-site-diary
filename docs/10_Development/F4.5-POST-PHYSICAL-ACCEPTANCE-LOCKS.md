4. **Phone anchors and rail termination (F45-SPINE-002).** Phone SUMBER aligns to its actual SUMBER section anchor without overshoot above the section. Phone SIMPAN aligns to the final Save section. The rail terminates intentionally at the final SIMPAN node, without premature stop or visual tail/overshoot below it. Replace it with a connected tactical pulse system, not a generic flat table. Keep project identity clearly above/with the pulse system. Motif supports meaning/readability, never decoration noise. F45-HDR-001/002, F45-GEO-001, F45-CSS-001 and F45-RESP-001 remain protected.
6. **Responsive tactical forecast placement (F45-WX-004).** WIDE >=1200: usable forecast is a compact secondary weather pulse at the end of the Tactical Pulse Bar. HALF 768-1199: four core facts remain dominant; weather moves to a compact secondary strip rather than crowding them. PHONE: four core facts remain dominant; weather appears as a compact secondary strip BELOW the core pulse, does not compete with SEMASA and causes no horizontal overflow.

Loading/unavailable remains visually secondary, never becomes a first-class metric and must not change or break core pulse geometry.
