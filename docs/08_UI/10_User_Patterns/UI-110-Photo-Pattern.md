# 1. Purpose

Defines the standard behaviour for capturing, managing and displaying construction photos throughout the JKR Site Diary Platform.

Photos are considered operational records and form part of the project audit trail.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Photo Pattern shall:

- Simplify photo capture.
- Maintain traceability.
- Preserve image quality.
- Support field operation.
- Support AI-assisted analysis.

---

# 3. Supported Operations

Capture

Upload

Preview

Annotate

Replace

Download

Archive

---

# 4. Metadata

Every photo shall store:

- Project
- Date
- Time
- User
- GPS (where available)
- Activity
- Description

---

# 5. Gallery Behaviour

Photos may be displayed as:

Grid

List

Timeline

Fullscreen

---

# 6. Mobile Behaviour

Camera shall open directly from supported devices.

Multiple photos may be captured before submission.

Uploads should continue in the background whenever possible.

---

# 7. AI Behaviour

Future AI capabilities may include:

Object Detection

Progress Recognition

Safety Observation

OCR Extraction

---

# 8. Accessibility

Images shall provide descriptive alternative text where appropriate.
