# 1. Purpose

Defines the standard calendar interaction used throughout the JKR Site Diary Platform.

Calendars organise operational activities based on time while preserving project context.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Calendar Pattern shall:

- Improve schedule visibility.
- Simplify date selection.
- Highlight operational milestones.
- Support planning and tracking.
- Integrate with programme schedules.

---

# 3. Calendar Views

Day

Week

Month

Agenda

Timeline (Future)

---

# 4. Calendar Events

Events may include:

Activities

Inspections

Meetings

Milestones

Approvals

Deadlines

Weather Alerts

---

# 5. Navigation

Users shall navigate using:

Previous

Next

Today

Date Picker

Jump To Date

---

# 6. Event States

Scheduled

In Progress

Completed

Cancelled

Delayed

Pending Approval

---

# 7. Interaction Rules

Selecting an event opens detailed information.

Dragging events is optional depending on permissions.

Read-only calendars shall prevent editing.

---

# 8. Mobile Behaviour

Calendar shall support swipe navigation.

Agenda View becomes the preferred mobile presentation.

---

# 9. AI Behaviour

Future AI may:

Recommend meeting dates.

Predict schedule clashes.

Highlight critical milestones.

Generate daily summaries.

---

# 10. Accessibility

Calendar shall support keyboard navigation.

Colour shall not be the sole event indicator.
