# Purpose

Audit stores an immutable history of all significant system events.

Audit provides complete traceability for operational, planning and administrative actions.

Audit records support compliance, investigation and reporting.

Audit never modifies business data.

# Business Rules

Audit records are append-only.

Audit records cannot be edited.

Audit records cannot be deleted.

Every approval action shall generate an Audit record.

Every Activity lifecycle transition shall generate an Audit record.

Audit shall preserve historical values.

Audit shall never become the source of business data.
