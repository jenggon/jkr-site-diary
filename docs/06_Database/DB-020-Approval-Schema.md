# Approval Schema

Status

Approved

---

# Purpose

Approval records the review and approval process for operational records within the Site Diary Platform.

Approval supports multi-level approval workflows while maintaining full auditability.

Approval does not modify operational data.

Approval only records approval decisions.

# Business Rules

Approval shall always belong to one Activity.

Approval may reference a Site Diary.

Approval may reference a Progress record.

Each approval decision is immutable after submission.

Rejected approvals require comments.

Returned approvals may be resubmitted.

Approval never changes operational records directly.
