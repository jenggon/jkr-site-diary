## 3. F2.6 chronology

### B01 — navigation recon and route contract

B01 inventoried the actual App Router pages, component-state destinations, API transports, and identity handoffs. It identified `/site-diary` as the only complete Programme-aware product workspace and escalated two product decisions rather than inventing them: root treatment and Approval visibility. HQ subsequently locked `/` to redirect to `/site-diary` and kept Approval contextual, without introducing a global capability-discovery API.

### B02 — canonical landing and navigation cleanup

B02 replaced the legacy root Activity surface with a server-side, non-mutating `redirect('/site-diary')`. It removed the generic ID-less Print navigation and retained exact Print links only where a persisted `siteDiaryId` exists. No global shell, authentication redesign, migration, or dependency was added.

### B03 — Programme and stale-context hardening

B03 made Programme selection the owner of current Revision resolution and dependent workspace state. Programme changes immediately clear Revision, dates, and Programme-owned errors; abort and generation ownership prevent late Programme A work from committing into Programme B. The workspace remounts Programme-owned child state while preserving the selected contextual tab category, and closes an exact Approval review when its Programme owner changes. A Programme with no current Revision remains explicitly unavailable and cannot inherit or fabricate the prior Programme's Revision.

### B04 — integration regression and contract finalization

B04 found no production defect. It completed the merged navigation contract documentation and ran the established F2.4/F2.5/F2.6 integration fixture successfully. Only documentation changed in B04.

## 4. Final canonical route contract

- `/` is a server redirect to `/site-diary`; it performs no product mutation.
- `/login` remains the authentication utility entry and its successful `/` destination resolves deterministically to the canonical workspace.
- `/site-diary` is the sole canonical primary product workspace.
- NEW, OPEN, RECORDS, and APPROVALS are contextual destinations within that workspace. Exactly one tab is marked active and its rendered child agrees with it.
- `/site-diary/print?id=<encoded siteDiaryId>` is the exact Print utility route.
- There is no global Approval destination and no generic ID-less Print destination.
- Standalone exact Diary and Approval deep-link routes are **DEFERRED / NON-REQUIREMENT** for the accepted F2.6 journey. Current and historical Diary detail/edit and Approval review remain safely reachable through Programme-owned component-state navigation. Loss of ephemeral state returns to a bounded workspace state; it does not remap exact identity.

## 5. Final identity hierarchy

The locked hierarchy is scope and validation context, never a reconstruction chain:

1. `programmeId` scopes Programme-owned navigation and data.
2. `revisionId` identifies an authorized current Revision for operational work or an explicitly selected historical Revision for read-only history.
3. `activityId` identifies canonical operational Activity state.
4. `siteDiaryId` is sovereign for persisted Diary detail, edit, history, and Print.
5. `approvalId` identifies the Approval and remains bonded to its canonical `siteDiaryId`.

Broader identities do not reconstruct narrower exact identities. Programme and Revision context validate an exact Diary or Approval; they do not replace its persisted ID.

## 6. Authority boundary

Navigation and client context do not infer authorization. Programme-scoped Approval queue reads, exact Approval review, Approval decisions, and exact Print continue through server/database authority. Static SQL contracts retain actor binding, Programme membership and capability checks, fail-closed behavior, hardened function boundaries, and exact Print read capability enforcement.

F2.6 did not weaken F2.4 Approval behavior: exact Approval/Diary bonding, pending-state validation, server-side capability enforcement, optimistic concurrency tokens, canonical lock ordering, and bounded `409`, `403`, and `404` handling remain covered. No client-side role or capability inference was introduced.

## 7. Historical behavior

RECORDS resolves current and historical Revision lists within the selected Programme. A selected historical Diary remains sovereign by its persisted `siteDiaryId`; detail, edit eligibility, history, and Print re-read or hand off that exact ID. Historical selection is cleared when Programme ownership changes. No current-Revision remapping or reconstruction from Programme, Revision, or Activity occurs.

## 8. Approval integration

Approval is a contextual workspace tab, not a global destination. Its queue is scoped by `programmeId`. Selecting an item passes the exact `approvalId` and bonded `siteDiaryId` into review. Review re-reads both exact records, verifies their Programme/Revision/Activity bond and Pending status, and submits a decision only against the exact Approval with the Diary concurrency token. A Programme change immediately removes prior review and decision controls.

## 9. Print integration

Generic ID-less Print navigation is absent. Current detail, historical detail, and post-save feedback hand off only:

`/site-diary/print?id=<encoded siteDiaryId>`

The Print client requires the query ID, calls `/api/site-diary/<encoded siteDiaryId>/print`, rejects identity mismatch, prevents stale requests from owning newer state, and has no canonical `/api/reports` fallback. F2.5 exact authorization, canonical snapshot semantics, renderer, and pagination behavior remain covered by the merged regression and full verification.

## 10. Navigation read-only invariant

The root redirect, Programme selection, contextual tab selection, record/review selection, and Print handoff do not mutate domain state. Mounted ownership tests confirm Programme navigation performs GET requests only. Domain mutations remain explicit workflow actions inside their existing authorized components and APIs; navigation itself does not create, update, approve, reject, return, or print-persist data.
